How Cosmic Echoes works

Each star creates smaller stars until it reaches the chosen depth. Branching sets the number of children, decay softens them, and the base delay and tempo shape their timing. Audio drives the visual timeline. WAV export renders the recorded notes offline.

Built with HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, Canvas 2D, Web Audio, OfflineAudioContext and PCM WAV encoding.
