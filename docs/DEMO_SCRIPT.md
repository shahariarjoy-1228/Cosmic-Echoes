# Demo Script (Provisional)

This outline describes the planned demo. Revise it after implementation and verification.

1. Introduce Cosmic Echoes as a relaxing space-themed soundscape. Start playback and place a root star.
2. Show the child echoes branching recursively. Change depth or the number of children and place another star to demonstrate the effect.
3. Let the synchronized visuals and ambient music play. Demonstrate keyboard controls and reduced motion.
4. Pause and resume to show synchronization, then stop and replay the saved composition.
5. Export a WAV and play a short excerpt to demonstrate the melody, ambient layer, and reverb tail. Reset the application.
