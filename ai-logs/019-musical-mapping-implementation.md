### Musical behavior

| Scale | Notes | Environment |
|---|---|---|
| C major pentatonic | C, D, E, G, A | Deep Space, default |
| A minor pentatonic | A, C, D, E, G | Nebula Dreams |
| F major pentatonic | F, G, A, C, D | Solar Harmony |

A root’s position selects a pitch from the middle of the available scale notes. At odd depths, child indices `0`, `1`, and `2` request **+2, −2, and 0 scale steps** from their parent. Even depths reverse the directions. Range boundaries can shorten the movement or repeat the parent note. Movement stays within a perfect fifth.

Onsets are copied exactly from the celestial events, so tempo is applied once by recursion. Musical duration uses celestial duration, radius, and depth and stays within **0.4–3 seconds**. Intensity follows celestial amplitude up to **0.70**, and the default stereo pan runs linearly from **−0.65 to +0.65**. Roots use `starlight`, children use `echo`, and the separate `nebula-pad` harmony supplies one low tonic and fifth per composition request.

For example, `generateEchoTree({ id: "star", x: 0.5, y: 0.5 }, { maxDepth: 0 }, 2)` produces one centered root. With default settings, its musical note references celestial ID `["star"]` and maps to **E4**, MIDI **64**, about **329.63 Hz**, at **2 seconds**, with a **1.344-second** duration, **0.70** intensity, centered pan, and no musical parent.
