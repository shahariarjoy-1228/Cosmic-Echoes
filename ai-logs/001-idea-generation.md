## User Prompt

I'm attaching the rules PDF for a 24-hour SOLO hackathon ("Recursive Soundscapes", Hub's Quest Epoch 1). Read the whole PDF carefully first, especially the mandatory deliverables, the evaluation weights, and the note about saving the prompt chain in the repo.

YOUR ROLE
Act as a creative-coding mentor and hackathon judge. Your job is IDEA GENERATION ONLY. Do not write any code yet.

MY CONSTRAINTS
- Solo, 24 hours, building mostly with AI coding agents.
- Browser-based (HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Canvas, Web Audio API). No servers, no paid APIs, no ML models, no large audio files.
- Beginner-to-intermediate level, so the idea must be low-risk to finish but still stand out.

TASK
1. Generate 10 distinct ideas, each built on a different recursive structure (fractal trees, Koch curve, Sierpinski, L-systems, Fibonacci, Towers of Hanoi, recursive ripples, Collatz, Mandelbrot/Julia, recursive rhythms, Cantor sets, or original ones of your own).
2. For each idea, give (under 120 words total):
   - Name and one-line pitch
   - The recursive function and its user-configurable parameters
   - Music mapping: how depth, branch, value or position maps to pitch, rhythm, harmony, timbre, sound effects and ambient layers
   - Visual concept and how it syncs with the sound
   - The relaxation hook
   - The "wow" factor a judge would remember
   - How audio export will work
   - Build difficulty (1-5) and the main technical risk
   - Estimated score per category using the PDF's evaluation weights, plus a total out of 100
3. Rank all ideas by (expected score) x (probability I finish in 24 hours with AI help).
4. Pick your top 3. Assume most participants will build a plain fractal tree with random notes, and explain what makes each of your top 3 different from that.
5. For the #1 pick, give me:
   - A 24-hour timeline with milestones
   - An MVP vs. nice-to-have list (what to cut if time runs short)
   - 3 ways to make it feel more original and polished (sound design, visuals, UX)
   - A 60-second demo script for the judges

RULES
- Prioritize relaxation and musical quality over algorithm complexity, since they carry the most weight in the PDF's scoring.
- Every sound must be pleasant by design (scales, slow tempo, soft timbres, reverb), never harsh or random.
- Be concrete and specific, with no vague buzzwords.
- End by asking me 3 short questions about my skills and preferences so you can refine the ideas.
